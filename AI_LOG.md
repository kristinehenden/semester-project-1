# AI Usage Log

AI has been used as a support tool throughout the project.

## Planning and design

I used ChatGPT to help me understand the project brief, organize the project tasks and discuss ideas for the website structure and design.

During the design process, I used AI to explore different visual directions, layouts and design ideas. I used the suggestions as inspiration and made my own decisions and adjustments while creating the final designs in Figma.

AI was also used to review parts of the design, including layout, readability, responsive design and accessibility.

## Images

The images used in my final design were generated with AI. I generated and tested several different images before choosing the ones that suited the content and visual direction of the website.

## Development

ChatGPT has been used to guide me through setting up the project structure, Git and GitHub.

This log will be updated if I use AI during the development and testing of the website.
